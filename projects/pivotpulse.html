PivotPulse

An NSE VCP stock scanner and market-monitoring system designed to support stock screening and technical analysis. The project focuses on identifying potential volatility contraction patterns and organizing market information to support further analysis.

View GitHub Repository Back to Projects

Overview

PivotPulse is a Python-based stock-scanning and market-monitoring project focused on the Indian equity market. It explores how technical screening rules can help narrow down stocks for closer examination.

The system is centered on the Volatility Contraction Pattern (VCP) concept, which examines price consolidation and contracting volatility as part of a technical analysis process.

The scanner is intended to assist analysis, not to provide guaranteed predictions or investment recommendations.

Project Objectives

  • Explore automated stock screening for the Indian market.
  • Apply technical analysis concepts associated with VCP patterns.
  • Reduce manual effort involved in reviewing candidate stocks.
  • Organize screening results for further market analysis.
  • Develop a reusable Python-based market-monitoring workflow.

Analysis Workflow

  • Obtain the required market data.
  • Prepare and inspect price data.
  • Apply the implemented stock-screening criteria.
  • Identify stocks that satisfy the configured conditions.
  • Review the results for further technical analysis.

The precise data sources, screening conditions and output format are defined by the implementation in the repository.
