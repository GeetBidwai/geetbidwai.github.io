Overview

AI-Analyst explores how AI-powered workflows can simplify research by coordinating tasks that would otherwise require manual searching, reviewing information, and organizing findings.

The project focuses on automating parts of the research process and presenting the resulting information in a more structured format.

Research Workflow

  • Initiate a research task.
  • Gather relevant information from available sources.
  • Organize research findings.
  • Generate a structured research output.

The exact workflow depends on the agents and integrations implemented in the repository.
